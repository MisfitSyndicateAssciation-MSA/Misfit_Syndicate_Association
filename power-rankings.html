⚠️ POWER RANKINGS ARE NOT THE STANDINGS

League standings remain the official record. The Syndicate Power Rankings are an automated measure of team strength based on results, scoring, playoff odds and momentum. The weekly movement tracker uses archived completed-week Power Ranking snapshots so the Syndicate can keep the receipts. In other words: you can be ranked below somebody you beat.
